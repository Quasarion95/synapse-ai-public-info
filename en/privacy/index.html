Privacy Policy

What happens to your data.

The web version and the phone apps work differently, so they are described separately below. Version of 15 October 2026.

Home Support Русская версия

1. In short

Your records — tasks, goals, lists, notes, finances — live on your device: in your browser's storage if you use the web version, or on the phone itself if you use the app. We don't create an account for you, don't ask for your name and don't ask for your phone number. The only things sent to the server are what the assistant needs to answer and what is needed to process a payment.

2. Web version

The web version at synapseapp.ru/app/ keeps your records in your browser's storage (localStorage) on this device. Until you turn on device sync (see section 3), there is no copy on the server: clear the site data, use private browsing or another browser — and the records won't be there. You can save a backup as a file in "Settings → Data".

The web version has no sign-in at all: we ask for neither your email nor your phone number. A subscription is recognised by a code you enter yourself. Sync with your other devices is optional — how it works is described in section 3.

In your profile you can add a name and a photo, so that you are addressed by name rather than as "user". This is optional, and they stay in the same place as your tasks: in your browser's storage. The name and photo are not sent to the server and are not passed to the assistant either. The phone apps have no name field at all.

The "My finances" section follows the same rules: spending and income, debts together with the names of the people they mention, savings jars, envelopes, subscriptions and recurring payments are kept in your browser's storage. The service does not connect to any bank accounts or cards and will not do so — you enter the numbers yourself. These records are not sent to the server; there is one exception, described in the section on the assistant.

3. Phone apps

On iPhone. Tasks, goals, stages, lists, notes, finances and settings are stored on the device, in a file that the system encrypts together with your passcode. You can move them between your devices with sync — see below.

The app may ask for access to notifications, the microphone, speech recognition and Face ID. Each permission is optional and can be turned off in iOS Settings. Biometrics are handled by the system itself; the app never sees them.

On Android. Records are stored inside the app itself and, by default, are not copied anywhere: there is no automatic cloud backup here. You can move them to another device with sync (see below) or by saving a file in "Settings → Data" — the same file also protects your records if the app is uninstalled. The only permission the app asks for is the microphone, and only at the moment you tap it in the assistant window; declining it doesn't break anything else. One difference from iPhone, stated plainly: there, records are kept in a file that the system encrypts together with the device passcode, while on Android they are stored inside the app — the system shields them from other apps, but they have no separate encryption. On an unlocked or reflashed phone this protection is weaker.

Device sync. Optionally — and only with a subscription — you can link devices with a short code so that tasks, goals, lists, notes and finances carry over between them: for example, between iPhone, the web version and Android. In this case records are copied through our server, but they travel there encrypted: the key is created on your devices at the moment of linking and never reaches the server, so all we hold is a block we cannot read, and we cannot see its contents. Until sync is turned on, none of this happens — records stay only on the device, as described above. You can turn sync off and delete the copy from the server at any time in settings; this does not touch the records on the device itself.

4. The Syn assistant

When you talk to Syn, the request goes to our server and from there to the model provider. Along with the text of your request, a slice of your workspace is sent: tasks and goals with their dates and titles, and in the web version also lists and notes. Without it the assistant wouldn't know what to reschedule or what you're asking about.

When you talk to Syn from the "My finances" section, money records are added to the slice: open debts with names and remaining amounts, the names of savings jars, subscriptions and recurring payments, the month's totals for spending and income, and the five largest categories. This is the only case in which finance records leave your browser, and it happens only when you make a request — the section itself sends nothing anywhere. If you don't want to share these figures with a third-party model provider, keep your finances without the assistant: the whole section works without it.

On iPhone you can reach Syn not only in the app but also by voice — from the Home Screen widget. Your voice is turned into text by Apple's system speech recognition; the app does not store the audio and does not send it to our server — only the resulting text is sent, just like a regular request.

If detailed context is on — and it is on by default — a request from the app or from the widget may also send the assistant your goals, tasks, Syn memory, basic progress indicators and finance records: spending, income, debts, subscriptions. As in the web version, if you don't want to share these figures with a third-party model provider, don't use Syn — finances and everything else in the app work without it.

We don't store this slice. What stays on the server: a daily request counter tied to an installation identifier; your subscription status and its term; records of completed payments; the conversation with the assistant itself and its memory between conversations — without them Syn wouldn't remember what was said a minute ago. The conversation and memory can be erased in settings. No tasks, goals, notes or finance records remain on the server.

5. Payment

Payments are accepted by YooKassa; card details are processed by YooKassa and never reach us. We store the fact of purchase, the subscription term, the access code as an irreversible hash and — if auto-renewal is on — the payment method identifier and the last four digits of the card, so we can show you exactly what is being renewed.

You can unlink the card and turn off auto-renewal yourself on the "Subscription" page (in Russian) using your access code.

6. Website and analytics

The synapseapp.ru website pages use Yandex Metrica: it counts visits and records anonymised sessions (Session Replay), so we can see where pages are unclear.

In the web version of the planner at /app/ Metrica also runs, but differently: screen recording is turned off there. On the website pages there is nothing personal to record — just ordinary text; your screen in the planner holds your tasks, notes and money, and we won't record them for the sake of any report. Only visits and moves between sections are counted — nothing you have written.

The iPhone and Android apps have no analytics counter at all, just as they have no advertising SDKs and no advertising identifier: they don't track you across other apps.

7. What you can delete and how

In the web version: "Settings → Data → Erase everything in this browser" — and no records remain anywhere, because they were never anywhere else. In the app: delete any record, erase the assistant's memory, delete the account entirely.

To have us delete what is stored on the server (subscription, counters, assistant memory), write to support@synapseapp.ru — we'll do it and confirm.
