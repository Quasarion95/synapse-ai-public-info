Create
the causes

The result is never fully in our hands. The effort is. You create the causes: you take a step, then another, and another. Synapse is there so those steps don't get lost and add up to a path.

Three things no to-do list has

01

A goal, not just tasks

A goal has stages, and stages have their own tasks. Progress counts itself, and you can see at once which task today really moves the big thing.

02

The Syn assistant

Just say it: "move the call to Friday", "sort out my day", "turn this into a goal". Syn changes the plan itself and shows exactly what it did.

All goals, stages and linked tasks on one canvas. You can see where things are moving and where nothing has happened for a month.

About your data — honestly

Your records stay with you

Tasks, goals, notes and money live on your device; from a phone a copy goes only to your own iCloud or Google backup. We don't collect them.

What the assistant sees

To answer, Syn gets a snapshot of your tasks, goals, lists and notes, and for the briefing and money questions — your finances too. You can erase the chat in settings.

No ads inside

No advertising SDKs in the apps. We don't sell data.

Encrypted sync

With Pro, sync moves your records between devices: they reach the server encrypted, and only you hold the key. Or move them as one file in "Settings → Data".
